Accessibility Statement

A Nomad's Consulting wants everyone to be able to explore their options for living and traveling abroad, including people with disabilities. This statement explains how we work toward that and how to reach us if something gets in your way.

Our standard

This statement covers the website A Nomad's Consulting at mfebres0919.github.io/Nomad-s-Consulting.

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content easier to use for people with disabilities, including people who are blind or have low vision, are deaf or hard of hearing, or have motor, cognitive, or learning disabilities. Meeting them also makes the site easier for everyone to use.

Conformance status

Partially conformant

This website is partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. That means most of the content meets the standard, but some parts do not yet fully conform. Those parts are listed under Known limitations.

Our efforts

We take the following steps to support accessibility:

  • Accessibility is part of how the site is designed and built from the start, not added afterward.
  • Text and buttons meet color contrast requirements, and text can be zoomed to 200% without losing content.
  • Every feature can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Images include text descriptions, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Moving content, such as the photo slideshows and the services carousel, can be paused, and animation is reduced for visitors who turn on "reduce motion" in their device settings.
  • Form fields have visible labels, and errors are explained in text.
  • We test pages with automated tools and by hand, and review accessibility whenever the site changes.

Known limitations

Despite our efforts, some content may not yet be fully accessible. Here's what we know about and what we're doing:

Third-party forms and booking tools

Issue: Our consultation questionnaire and booking tools are provided by outside services and may not fully meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

Why: We can't fully control the code of tools we don't build.

What we're doing: We choose providers that support accessibility and test their forms before launch.

In the meantime: Email us and we'll complete the questionnaire with you by email or phone.

Travel photos

Issue: Some personal travel photos are described briefly and may not capture every detail.

What we're doing: We review image descriptions whenever new photos are added.

In the meantime: Contact us if a photo's description isn't helpful, and we'll improve it.

Technical details

Compatibility

This website is designed to work with recent versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge on desktop and mobile, along with common assistive technologies such as VoiceOver (Mac and iPhone), NVDA (Windows), and TalkBack (Android). It may not work as well in browsers more than three major versions old.

Technologies used

Accessibility on this site relies on the following technologies working with your browser and any assistive technology you use:

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • WAI-ARIA
  • SVG

How we assessed this site

We evaluated this website through self-evaluation: automated testing with Google Lighthouse and axe, plus manual checks of keyboard navigation, screen reader behavior, color contrast, zoom, and reduced-motion settings.

Feedback & contact

We welcome your feedback. If you run into a barrier on this site, or need information in a different format, please let us know. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

  • Email aahniyah@anomadsconsulting.com
  • Contact form Send a message from our homepage

Formal complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, please email us with "Accessibility complaint" in the subject line. The founder reviews every accessibility complaint personally and will reply with how we plan to resolve it.

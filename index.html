Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm Aahniyah. This all started with my own move.

In 2025, I took a gap year to travel the world, explore new cultures, and figure out where I could see myself settling down. I vlogged along the way, not as a tourist, but to understand what everyday life actually felt like in each country. What started as a search for my forever home turned into hopping from country to country and falling in love with the freedom of living abroad.

Eventually, I established residency in Brazil, now my part-time home. Going through it myself taught me how hard it can be to navigate unfamiliar systems, find reliable information, and connect with the right people, and I built relationships with professionals who can make the move easier for you.
